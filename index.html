About

A blend of technical, financial, and business judgment.

I've spent three decades inside organizations where operations, technology, and money all have to work together — power utilities, life sciences, and financial services. My work has ranged from running global IT operations across four continents to negotiating the sale of a telecommunications business, from rebuilding a failing service delivery model to winning a first-of-its-kind FCC waiver.

What ties it together is a track record of taking on complicated, high-stakes projects — service delivery transformations, contract disputes, technology migrations, M&A analysis — and bringing them in on time, on budget, and with a result the business can measure. I'm now offering that same judgment as an independent advisor to organizations navigating similar decisions.
